# Journey Brief — Emerald Technology Consulting

## Business Summary
Emerald Technology Consulting helps time-poor business owners make more money by cutting through tech complexity — AI, automation, notes, and systems — so they stop drowning and start scaling.

## Concept Spine
**"From Tech Overwhelm to Tech Clarity"** — the viewer descends through a dark, foggy expanse where scattered points of light (gems of insight) gradually resolve into a clear, illuminated path forward.

## Spatial Model
A conceptual 3D space — not a literal cave, but an **atmospheric void** with depth layers:

```
    ┌────────────── LAYER 1 (Entry) ─────────────────┐
    │  Foggy dark void, faint emerald glimmers        │
    │  No structure — just depth and particles        │
    └────────────────────┬────────────────────────────┘
                         │ auto-scroll forward
    ┌────────────────────┴────────────────────────────┐
    │  LAYER 2 (The Gems)                              │
    │  Floating emerald nodes in 3D space              │
    │  Nodes are interactive — click to expand insight │
    └────────────────────┬────────────────────────────┘
                         │ scroll forward
    ┌────────────────────┴────────────────────────────┐
    │  LAYER 3 (The Door)                              │
    │  Bright opening — light pouring in               │
    │  Clean, minimal — the path is clear              │
    └─────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Adjacency:**
| From | Direction | To |
|------|-----------|-----|
| Layer 1 (Overwhelm) | Forward, auto-scroll | Layer 2 (The Gems) |
| Layer 2 (The Gems) | Forward, scroll-drive | Layer 3 (The Door) |

**Entry point:** Dark fog, camera at mid-depth looking into the void.
**Exit point:** Bright aperture, camera passes through the light.
**Constraint:** No literal cave walls, no stalactites, no dungeon aesthetic. Space must feel infinite, not enclosed.

## Camera Path
### Scene 1 — The Overwhelm (0–33% scroll)
- **Start:** Camera floating in deep darkness. Faint emerald particles drift in from the upper-right.
- **Movement:** Very slow forward drift. Particles resolve into a rough emerald crystal shape centered in frame.
- **Transition:** Camera pushes past the emerald crystal — it dissolves into floating shards that become the gem nodes of Scene 2.
- **Headline overlay:** "Make more money with technology"
- **Subtext:** "Cut through the noise. Let's build your path."

### Scene 2 — The Gems (33–66% scroll)
- **Start:** Camera emerges into a field of floating emerald nodes suspended in dark space. Each node is a hexagonal/octahedral faceted gem.
- **Movement:** Camera glides slowly through the node field. Nodes drift into readable positions.
- **Interaction:** Nodes are clickable — each expands to reveal tutorial content (collapsed → expanded card).
- **Transition:** The node field parts — a bright aperture appears in the distance, getting larger.

### Scene 3 — The Door (66–100% scroll)
- **Start:** Bright aperture growing in frame. Camera approaches.
- **Movement:** Steady approach toward the light. Details resolve — it's a clean, minimal emerald-hued interface area with contact form.
- **End:** Camera passes through the aperture. Full brightness. Contact form in the clear space.
